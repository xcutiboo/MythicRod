[CENTER][COLOR=#23313f]MythicRod replaces vanilla fishing with configurable loot tables, legendary items, biome-specific drops, live statistics, and an intuitive in-game GUI.[/COLOR][/CENTER]

[CENTER][SIZE=6][COLOR=#f6c45f]━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━[/COLOR][/SIZE][/CENTER]

[CENTER][IMG]https://i.ibb.co/4ZHZmWwr/FEATURES.png[/IMG][/CENTER]
[LIST]
[*][B][COLOR=#f6c45f]Unlimited Custom Drops[/COLOR][/B] — Materials, weights, and amounts per table.
[*][B][COLOR=#f6c45f]Biome-Specific Loot[/COLOR][/B] — Unique rewards per biome to encourage exploration.
[*][B][COLOR=#f6c45f]Legendary Items[/COLOR][/B] — Custom names, lore, enchants, and glow.
[*][B][COLOR=#f6c45f]Statistics & Leaderboards[/COLOR][/B] — Track catches, rares, legends, and top fishers.
[*][B][COLOR=#f6c45f]In-Game GUI[/COLOR][/B] — Intuitive configuration menus, statistics viewers, and language selector.
[*][B][COLOR=#f6c45f]Multi-Language Support[/COLOR][/B] — English and Japanese built-in with modern language selector GUI (flag-textured heads).
[*][B][COLOR=#f6c45f]Permissions[/COLOR][/B] — Gate drops by rank or group.
[*][B][COLOR=#f6c45f]Effects[/COLOR][/B] — Sounds and particles for rare/legendary catches.
[*][B][COLOR=#f6c45f]Performance[/COLOR][/B] — Async handling, optimized hook tracking, and memory-efficient design for large servers.
[*][B][COLOR=#f6c45f]Modern Paper API[/COLOR][/B] — Built for Paper 1.21.4+ with zero deprecated APIs and modern player profile system.
[*][B][COLOR=#f6c45f]Developer API[/COLOR][/B] — Extensible plugin API for third-party integrations.
[/LIST]

      name: '&b&lExcalibur'
      lore:
        - '&7A legendary blade'
      enchantments:
        sharpness: 10
      glowing: true

biome-drops:

    - 'HEART_OF_THE_SEA,5,1'

  jungle:
